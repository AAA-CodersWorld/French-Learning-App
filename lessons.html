Lesson 1: Greetings (Les Salutations)

Start with the most common greetings used in everyday French.

  • Bonjour — Hello / Good morning
  • Bonsoir — Good evening
  • Salut — Hi (informal)
  • Au revoir — Goodbye
  • Bonne nuit — Good night

Pronunciation Tips

Bon sounds like “bon” in “bonfire” but softer.

Jour sounds like “zhoor”.

Listen & Repeat

Bonjour — Hello / Good morning

Salut — Hi (informal)

Bonsoir — Good evening

Bonne nuit — Good night

Au revoir — Goodbye
